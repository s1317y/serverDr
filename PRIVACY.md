# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** (add date at release)

ServerDr does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

## What the app does and doesn't do

- **No account, no sign-in, no backend.** ServerDr has no servers of its own.
- **No analytics or telemetry.** No usage tracking, crash reporting or advertising SDKs are included.
- **Direct connections only.** The app connects from your device straight to the servers you configure, over SSH/SFTP.

## Data stored on your device

- **Server profiles** (name, host, port, username, settings) are stored locally on your device.
- **Passwords, private keys and passphrases** are stored using Android's secure, Keystore-backed storage and are never sent anywhere by the app.
- **Trusted SSH host fingerprints**, preferences, command favorites and recent commands are stored locally.

You can delete this data at any time by deleting a server profile, removing trusted hosts in Settings, or uninstalling the app.

## Data you choose to share

- **Exports** (such as security reports) are only created when you tap Export and go through Android's share sheet. ServerDr does not send them anywhere itself.
- **The in-app browser** loads whatever URLs you enter. Those sites are subject to their own privacy policies.

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this repository.
